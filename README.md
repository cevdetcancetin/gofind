## 🔒 Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 27, 2026

Go Find! is a party game where you hunt for real-world objects with your friends. This policy explains what data we process, why, and your rights.

### 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the personal data of people who use the Go Find! mobile app (the "App") is processed in accordance with Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") and related legislation. By using the App, you confirm that you have read this policy.

### 2. Account and Identity

The App does not ask for an account, email address or phone number. So that you can join games, your device is assigned a random, anonymous user ID (Firebase Anonymous Authentication). This ID is not linked to your real identity.

### 3. Data We Process

- Nickname and avatar: The name and animal avatar you choose when joining a room. Other people in the room can see them. Your last used name and avatar are stored only on your device so they're ready next time.
- Game photos: Photos you take with the app's camera and send during the game. Photos are downscaled before being sent.
- Game data: Room code, scores, round results, team and elimination info.
- Report records: When you report a photo, the room code, the photo and the related player IDs.
- Subscription info: Whether you have an active Go Find! Pro subscription is read from the purchase record that Apple verifies on your device. We never see your payment details; payment is handled entirely by Apple.

### 4. Who Sees the Photos

The room's leader sees the photo you send in order to approve it. Approved photos are shown to the players in the room in the gallery at the end of the game. Photos are not shared with anyone outside the room, not published publicly, and not used for advertising or AI training.

### 5. Retention Period

Game photos are deleted from the server when the game ends and the leader leaves the final screen. If a game is abandoned, photos expire after 6 hours, and rooms, player records and round results after 24 hours; expired records are deleted from the server shortly afterwards by automatic cleanup. Report records are kept as long as needed to review abuse and are deleted once the review is complete.

### 6. Where Data Is Stored

Game data and photos are stored on Google's Firebase infrastructure (Cloud Firestore, Europe region). Your data is therefore transferred to servers outside Türkiye within the scope of Article 9 of the KVKK.

### 7. Camera Permission

The camera is used only to photograph the object during the game. To prevent cheating, photos can't be picked from your library and your library is never accessed. You can turn off camera access anytime in iOS Settings > Go Find!

### 8. Ads and Tracking

The App shows no ads, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell your personal data. No third-party analytics or advertising tools are used.

### 9. Photos of Other People

When taking photos, make sure other people's faces or personal information don't appear without their permission. If you see an inappropriate photo, press and hold it in the gallery to report it; the reported photo is removed from your screen immediately.

### 10. Children's Privacy

The App is meant for families and groups of friends. Children under 13 should use the App under the supervision of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; using a nickname instead of a real name is recommended.

### 11. Data Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Server access rules ensure that only people in the room can read room data and only the leader can write scores.

### 12. Your Rights Under KVKK

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you have the right to learn whether your data is processed, request information, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object. Your photos are deleted automatically at the end of the game and your other game data within 24 hours; if you want them deleted sooner, write to us with your room code.

### 13. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated. Important changes are announced in the app; the current text is always on this screen.
